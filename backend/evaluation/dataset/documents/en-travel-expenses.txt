Travel and Expense Policy

Booking travel

Employees book flights and hotels through the company travel portal. Economy class is the default for flights under six hours; business class requires approval from a director.

Per diem

Meals during business travel are covered by a daily per diem of 60 US dollars for domestic trips and 80 US dollars for international trips. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

Receipts

Keep an itemized receipt for every expense over 25 US dollars. Photos of receipts are accepted if the amount, date, and vendor are readable.

Submitting claims

Submit expense claims in the finance system within 30 days of the end of the trip. Claims submitted later than 90 days are rejected. Reimbursement is paid with the next monthly payroll.

Corporate cards

Holders of a corporate card must reconcile card transactions every month, even if no claim is made.
