# Office Guide

## Opening hours

The office is open from 8:00 to 20:00 on weekdays. Badge access is required after 19:00, and the last person to leave turns off the lights in the kitchen.

## Meeting rooms

Book meeting rooms in the shared calendar. Rooms that are booked but unused for 15 minutes are released automatically so that others can use them.

## Working from home

Questions about working from home are answered by your manager; this guide only covers the office itself. Desks are not assigned, so clear your desk at the end of each day.

## Visitors

Visitors must be registered at reception and accompanied by an employee at all times.
